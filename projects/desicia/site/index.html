Where AI and analytics projects stall

A model gets built and a dashboard ships, yet no decision changes. The value sits in the step after the insight.

  • No path into the systems

    The output stops at a chart and never reaches SAP, Salesforce or the ticket queue.

  • No measure of success

    Nobody wrote down the number that would prove it worked.

Questions buyers ask

Our data is messy. Can we start in week 1?

Yes. Weeks 1 and 2 audit the data, and the first system uses only sources that pass.

Which customers have you served?

None we can name yet. You get a written diagnosis in two weeks and can stop there.

Do you replace our data team?

We build with them, and they own the system at handover.

Which models run the automation?

Open models in your cloud, or Azure OpenAI, Anthropic or Google on your own accounts.

How is pricing set?

A fixed price per phase, agreed in writing. No hourly billing.

Where is the team based?

Remote-first, with teams in North America, Europe and Asia.
