Privacy

Last updated 27 November 2026. This page is a working draft for the client to confirm with counsel.

What we collect

Only what you type into the consultation form: your name, work email, company, the area you chose and your message. The site sets no tracking cookies and loads no analytics.

Why

To reply to your request and arrange a call. We do not sell or share it, and we do not use it to train models.

Where it is kept

Requests are emailed to our team and stored in a request log on our server. We delete them 24 months after our last contact, or sooner when you ask.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about you through the consultation form on the home page. Residents of the EU and UK have the rights set out in the GDPR.

Third parties

The site loads nothing from other companies: fonts and scripts are served from our own server.
