## Teardown
### Palantir — palantir.com
- h1/positioning: platform names (AIP, Foundry), news-led; "Get Started".
- Proof: news, government and enterprise deployments; product codenames.
- Visual: near-black, white type, reel stills; carousel of announcements.
- Weak for us: a news carousel suits a public company, not a new firm; codenames need fame.

### Databricks — databricks.com
- h1 "One database for AI, apps and agents"; two CTAs (Explore the product / See demo).
- Proof: very large logo marquee "Trusted by data + AI teams".
- Section order: hero → logos → pill tabs per platform area → customer stories.
- Weak: generic "platform" copy, marquee as the main trust device.

### Scale AI — scale.com, scale.com/enterprise
- h1 "The world's most important decisions need reliable AI systems." /enterprise: "Reliable AI for the decisions your business runs on", "Schedule a consult".
- Thesis: "Most AI deployments in enterprise and government fail. We find the right use case, build the system, and own the outcome." "Humans stay in the loop."
- Proof: 93% faster contract reviews, $64M+ impact, six weeks to production; testimonials and logos.
- Strongest copy in the set: names the enemy (pilots that never ship) and the mechanism (own the outcome).
- Weak: proof-heavy layout we cannot copy without proof; dark, generic imagery.

### Snowflake — snowflake.com/en
- h1 "The Agentic Enterprise Runs on Snowflake".
- Hero: an authentic governance diagram with real system labels (ROLE: MARKETING_MANAGER, BUDGET: 20% used, RBAC).
- Weak: a clichéd 3-icon row (Easy / Connected / Trusted) further down.
- Take: show the system's real nouns instead of abstract art.

### Distyl — distyl.ai
- h1 "Architecting the AI-Native Enterprise"; forward-deployed engineers and researchers "who own the outcome".
- Proof: 150M+ end users, Fortune 500s, "measurable outcomes in weeks / bottom-line impact in months".
- Contact: mailto only. Motion: letter-by-letter thesis text.
- Weak: invented capitalised concept ("AI-Native Enterprise"), mailto-only conversion.

### Faculty — faculty.ai
- "Frontier AI for the frontlines of the world." A Sam Altman quote, NHS and energy cases, "Get in touch".
- Weak: slogan h1, borrowed celebrity quote.

### QuantumBlack (McKinsey)
- "Hybrid intelligence", "unlock the power", "Transform faster. Innovate smarter."; abstract blue flowing curves.
- The anti-pattern reference: every cliché in one place.

### Fractal / Tiger Analytics / Quantiphi
- Fractal: "Redesigning how enterprises run with AI", news carousel. Tiger: award/ISG carousel, cookie wall, "We provide certainty by solving your toughest challenges". Quantiphi: only a cookie wall rendered for a crawler.
- Weak: carousels, award walls, cookie walls before content.

### Linear — linear.app
- Real product UI is the hero (issue, activity log, agent actions); "Fig 0.1" figure labels; short, plain copy.
- Take: the product itself as the proof; one focal UI per screen.

### x.ai
- Dark ground, terminal/code UI, counters; two paths ("Build on your own" / "Get extra support").
- Take: two clear paths; avoid: dark navy + electric blue.

## What the top 1% do to convert
1. The h1 names what is sold and to whom; the sub-line names the mechanism (Scale: "build the system and own the outcome").
2. They name the enemy the buyer already feels: pilots that stall before production.
3. The product/system itself is the hero picture (Linear, Snowflake): real nouns, real states, no abstract art.
4. One primary action repeated word for word; a lower-commitment second path (demo, docs).
5. Proof sits next to the claim it supports — and where there is no proof, the best firms show the mechanism and the terms (time to production, who owns what).

## Category clichés (avoid)
- Logo marquees, news carousels, award walls, cookie walls before content.
- Abstract blue flowing curves, glowing brains, purple-blue gradients, particle fields, dark navy + electric blue.
- The 3-icon pillar row (Easy / Connected / Trusted), cards in cards.
- Copy: "transform", "unlock", "harness", "AI-native", "hybrid intelligence", "innovate smarter", "revolutionize", "synergy".
- Letter-by-letter text animation, counters on invented numbers, stock people at screens.

## White space (nobody says or shows this)
- **One team across the whole loop**: data → decision → automated action → measured result. Platforms sell the first half, consultancies advise on the second, agent shops automate one task. Nobody shows the loop end to end on their homepage.
- **Terms as proof**: nobody publishes their engagement terms (written success metric, code ownership, exit after any phase, data never leaving the client's cloud).
- **A working demo on the homepage** a buyer can operate (approve an automated action, change ROI inputs). Competitors show reels and stills.
- **A warm, light, paper-and-ink page** in a category of black and blue.

## Buyer objections we must answer
| Objection | Where the page answers it |
|---|---|
| Pilots never reach production | Thesis section; 12-week plan; "in production" wording |
| Our data is not ready | FAQ; weeks 1–2 data audit |
| You are new, no references | FAQ admission; written terms; demo you can operate |
| Security, data leaving our cloud | Security section: your cloud, no training on your data, audit log |
| Lock-in | You own code, models and prompts; exit after any phase |
| Black-box automation | People approve consequential actions; demo shows the approval |

## Pricing norms
Big consultancies: 3–18 months at $250–700/hour, change management often advisory only. Agent/FDE shops: fixed-scope 2–6 week builds. Nobody in the direct set publishes prices; "scoped after a call" is the norm and matches the brief.

## Positioning & Differentiation Brief
- **Category:** enterprise AI engineering firm — analytics, automation and the operating change around them.
- **For:** COOs, CFOs and CIOs at enterprise and high-growth companies, worldwide, whose AI work stalls between the dashboard and the business.
- **Enemy:** the stalled pilot — a model or dashboard that never changes a decision or a system.
- **Mechanism:** one team builds the whole loop — data → decision → automated action → measured result — inside the client's cloud.
- **Proof without proof:** written terms (success metric before build, fixed price per phase, client owns everything, exit after any phase), a demo the buyer can operate, the security model in plain words, and one honest admission ("we are new").
- **Positioning sentence (proposed, for client approval):** DECISIA is the AI engineering firm that takes one business decision from raw data to an automated, measured action in production — in the client's cloud, under the client's ownership.
- **Look:** paper and ink with a deep emerald accent; real system UI as every picture; one dark band for the demo.
